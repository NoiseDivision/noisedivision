# Noise Division v2.7.1

## TRANSPLANT 1.3.1
- HEAR ON: Club and Car no longer distort on loud masters. Their bigger low end used to push peaks into a soft clipper; now an automatic trim turns the preview down instead (shown as TRIM next to the preview warning), and the waveform is left alone.
- HEAR ON: new MONO choice. It folds the whole sound to mono, at the same level, and changes nothing else.

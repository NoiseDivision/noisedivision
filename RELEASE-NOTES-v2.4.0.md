# Noise Division v2.4.0

## New: TRANSPLANT 1.0.0, a section-aware match EQ
- Matches each section of your track to the same section of a reference: your drop to its drop, your break to its break. The curve follows the song as it plays.
- Key-aware low end: the reference's bass notes are moved into your key before comparing, so the curve doesn't boost its notes and cut yours.
- The curve is editable points: drag, add, remove. SMOOTH goes from surgical (1/24 octave) to broad strokes.
- LISTEN to the reference's matching part at the same moment, LEVEL MATCHED to your track.
- METERS: short-term and section loudness, true peak, PLR, width and tonal balance of both tracks, inside a typical EDM range.
- Linear or natural phase, up to three references, A/B, undo, 7 presets.
- VST3 and AU (no standalone app: it works inside a DAW).

SPECTRAKILL

Overview

What it does

SPECTRAKILL is a spectral compressor. A normal compressor turns the whole sound down when it gets loud. SPECTRAKILL splits the sound into hundreds of frequency bins and compresses each one on its own, so only the frequencies that poke above the threshold are pushed down, at the moment they do.

That makes it good at the things an EQ can't do: harshness that comes and goes, resonances that ring on some notes but not others, boomy bass notes, cymbal spikes. With UP it also works the other way, lifting quiet detail and room sound that sits below the threshold. A sidechain input lets another track decide what gets pushed down.

  • DOWN and UP: push down what pokes above the threshold and lift what sits below it, frequency by frequency
  • Threshold curve with up to 8 draggable points, plus TILT and SHARPNESS
  • ATTACK and RELEASE from 0.5 ms to 1 second, with a transient guard that keeps hits clean
  • Five quality settings, from 11 ms low latency for drums to ULTRA for full mixes
  • Stereo, L/R and M/S modes and a sidechain input for ducking a bass under the kick
  • 12 factory presets with descriptions, including Drum Detail, Harsh Killer and Sidechain Duck

Quick start

Up and running

  1. Pick a preset close to your source (Harsh Killer for vocals, Hi-Hat Tamer for cymbals, Bass Control for 808s).
  2. Drag the red threshold line down until the dark red area shows up where the sound is harsh.
  3. Set DOWN for how hard those parts are pushed down. Add UP to lift quiet detail.
  4. Double-click the display to add a point and bend the threshold, so only one area is touched.
  5. Pick a QUALITY: LOW LAT or NORMAL for drums, HIGH for most things, V.HIGH or ULTRA for bass and full mixes.

Reference

Every control

Compression

THRESHOLD−60 – 0 dB · default −24 dB

The level where SPECTRAKILL starts working, for every frequency. Drag the red line on the display or turn the knob.

DOWN0 – 100% · default 50%

How hard frequencies above the threshold are pushed down. 100% holds them at the threshold.

UP0 – 100% · default 0%

Lifts quiet frequencies below the threshold towards it (upward compression). Never lifts more than 24 dB, and leaves silence alone.

ATTACK0.5 – 200 ms · default 10 ms

How fast a frequency gets pushed down once it crosses the threshold.

RELEASE10 – 1000 ms · default 120 ms

How fast a frequency comes back once it drops below the threshold.

Threshold shape

TILT−6 – +6 dB/oct · default 0

Tips the threshold around 1 kHz. Right hits the highs harder, left hits the lows harder.

SHARPNESS0 – 100% · default 50%

How focused the curve points are. Low makes wide, gentle bends; high makes narrow, targeted ones.

Curve pointsup to 8

Double-click the display to add a point, drag it to bend the threshold there, double-click or right-click it to remove it.

Output

GAIN−24 – +24 dB · default 0

Output level after the processing.

MIX0 – 100% · default 100%

Blends the untouched sound back in, for parallel compression.

CLIPOFF · SOFT · HARD · default OFF

SOFT rounds off peaks above −0.1 dBFS; HARD cuts them flat there.

Routing and quality

STEREO · L / R · M / Sdefault STEREO

STEREO processes both channels together so the image stays put. L / R processes left and right separately. M / S processes the middle and the sides separately.

SIDECHAINOn / Off

The compression follows another track's spectrum (route it to SPECTRAKILL's sidechain input in your DAW). Where that track is loud, the same frequencies are pushed down here.

QUALITYLOW LAT · NORMAL · HIGH · V.HIGH · ULTRA · default HIGH

Higher settings resolve the frequencies more finely but respond more slowly and add more latency (about 11, 21, 43, 85 and 171 ms).

Display

X-ray

The bright bone is the output, the dashed ghost line is the input, and the dark dried blood between them is what SPECTRAKILL takes away. Fresh blood runs down from the top as deep as the cut.

SettingLatencyBest for
LOW LATENCY≈ 11 msFastest response, coarse frequency detail. Drums and live use.
NORMAL≈ 21 msDrums, hats and percussion.
HIGH≈ 43 msThe all-rounder: vocals, guitars, synths.

The latency is reported to your DAW, which delays everything else to match, so tracks stay in time on playback.

Sidechain

Duck by frequency

Classic sidechain turns the whole bass down on every kick. SPECTRAKILL only turns down the frequencies the kick is actually using.

  1. Put SPECTRAKILL on the bass (or whatever should make room) and load the Sidechain Duck preset.
  2. Route the kick (or vocal) to SPECTRAKILL's sidechain input. In FL Studio: right-click the kick's mixer track's send arrow to the bass track and choose Sidechain to this track, then pick it as the sidechain input in the plugin's wrapper. In Ableton Live and Logic: choose the kick in the plugin's Sidechain menu.
  3. Turn on SIDECHAIN. The bass now dips only at the frequencies where the kick is loud, only while it plays, instead of the whole bass pumping.

Tips

Getting the most out of it

  • Fast ATTACK (under 5 ms) catches spikes; slower ATTACK lets the hit through and works on the ring after it.
  • On a full mix, keep DOWN low (20–40%) and use ULTRA quality. It should sound like the mix got smoother, not quieter.
  • Use M / S and push the sides harder to tame wide, harsh cymbals without touching the centre.
  • If drums lose punch, use a lower QUALITY setting: it responds faster.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Windows

  1. Unzip

    Open the downloaded .zip. Inside is the plugin (.vst3) and a standalone app.

  2. Copy the .vst3

    Move the .vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. Every Windows DAW looks there.

  3. Rescan

    Let your DAW scan for new plugins. The plugin shows up under Noise Division.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. VST3 host or the standalone app.

macOS

  1. Open the installer

    Double-click the .pkg. It installs the Audio Unit, the VST3 and the standalone app.

  2. If macOS blocks it

    Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. This shows once, because the installers aren't from the App Store.

  3. Open your DAW

    Logic and GarageBand find the AU on their next start. Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig and Studio One use the VST3.

macOS 10.13 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel. AU or VST3 host, or the standalone app.

Help

Troubleshooting

The sidechain does nothing

Check that SIDECHAIN is on and the other track is routed to the plugin's sidechain input. Some DAWs need the plugin's sidechain input picked in the plugin wrapper.

It adds a lot of delay

Higher QUALITY settings add more latency. Your DAW compensates on playback; when recording live, use LOW LAT.

The plugin doesn't show up

Check the .vst3 is in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 (Windows) and rescan. On a Mac, run the installer again and restart the DAW.

No name, maker or category in FL Studio

Rescan with Verify plugins ticked in the plugin manager.

macOS says the installer can't be opened

System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. The installers aren't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet.

Windows SmartScreen warns about the app

Click More info, then Run anyway. This only concerns the standalone app.

The window is too big or too small

Drag the bottom-right corner. Every plugin scales from 60% to 200% and remembers the size.

Something else is wrong

E-mail noisedivision@proton.me with the plugin name, version, your DAW and your operating system.

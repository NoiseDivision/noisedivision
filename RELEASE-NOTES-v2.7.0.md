# Noise Division v2.7.0

## TRANSPLANT 1.3.0
- FULLNESS (in MATCH, per section): gives back the bass and air your ear stops hearing when music is quieter, from the equal-loudness curves (ISO 226). The quieter a section is than your loudest one, the more it gets, so breakdowns can feel as full as the drop. The display shows the whole EQ as a dashed line, and LEVEL MATCH takes it into account.
- HEAR ON (bottom left): hear your track, and the reference with REF, roughly as on a club system, in a car, on a laptop, a phone or earbuds, or in a quiet room, each at its usual level. Levelled in the mids so you compare tone, not volume. For your ears only: it is never in an offline bounce, and it goes back to Studio when a project opens.

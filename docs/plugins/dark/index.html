Overview

What it does

DARK is a one-knob darkener for drums and bass. Turning the DARKNESS knob moves several processes together: a tilt EQ that tips the highs down and the lows up, added low-end weight, tape-style saturation, and a low-pass filter that closes as you go further.

Darkening usually kills the attack of drums. DARK's PUNCH control opens the filter for a moment on every hit, so the transients stay bright and the tails, cymbals and room sink into the dark. It has zero latency, so it works while recording and on live drums.

  • One DARKNESS knob moves a tilt EQ, low-end weight, tape-style saturation and a closing low-pass together
  • PUNCH keeps the attack of every hit bright while tails and cymbals go dark
  • Mono low end: bass below 120 Hz folds to the centre as it darkens, so it stays solid on big systems
  • Zero latency, so it works on live drums and while recording
  • Advanced drawer with punch, mix and output

Under the hood

How it works

Everything below moves together when you turn the knob, in this order.

  1. Tilt EQ

    Tips the spectrum: highs down, lows up, by up to 7 dB at full DARKNESS.

  2. Low shelf

    Adds weight to the low end as DARKNESS goes up.

  3. Saturation

    Tape-style, slightly asymmetric saturation adds warmth and density, with anti-aliasing built in.

  4. Low-pass

    A resonant low-pass that closes from 20 kHz down to about 2.2 kHz at full DARKNESS; PUNCH opens it on every hit.

  5. Mono bass

    Below 120 Hz the sides fold into the centre as it darkens.

Quick start

Up and running

  1. Put DARK on a drum loop, drum bus or bass.
  2. Turn DARKNESS up. Low settings tilt and warm; past halfway it adds weight and closes the top.
  3. Open ADVANCED and set PUNCH: higher keeps the hits brighter.
  4. Use OUTPUT to make up any level it lost, and the candle to compare on and off.

Reference

Every control

Main

DARKNESS0 – 100% · default 50%

How dark the sound gets. Low settings tilt the highs down and add warmth; higher settings add weight and saturation and close the top; near 100% it sounds like it is coming through a wall. Double-click to return to 50%.

Candle (ON / BYPASS)On / Off

Light or snuff the candle: switches DARK on or off to compare with the untouched sound.

Advanced drawer

PUNCH0 – 100% · default 60%

Lets the hits through. The top opens for a moment on every attack (by up to 2.5 octaves), so drums keep their snap while the tails go dark. 0% darkens everything evenly.

MIX0 – 100% · default 100%

Blends the untouched sound back in.

OUTPUT−12 – +12 dB · default 0

Final level. Darker can sound a little quieter; turn this up to make up for it.

Every plugin window scales from 60% to 200%: drag the bottom-right corner. Double-click a knob to return it to its default.

Tips

Getting the most out of it

  • On a drum loop, try DARKNESS 40–60% with PUNCH around 70%: the kick and snare stay sharp while the hats and room move back.
  • On an 808 or reese bass, high DARKNESS with PUNCH at 0% gives a heavy, rounded tone that sits under everything.
  • Use MIX around 50% for parallel darkening: weight and warmth without losing the original top.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Windows

  1. Unzip

    Open the downloaded .zip. Inside is the plugin (.vst3) and a standalone app.

  2. Copy the .vst3

    Move the .vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. Every Windows DAW looks there.

  3. Rescan

    Let your DAW scan for new plugins. The plugin shows up under Noise Division.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. VST3 host or the standalone app.

macOS

  1. Open the installer

    Double-click the .pkg. It installs the Audio Unit, the VST3 and the standalone app.

  2. If macOS blocks it

    Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. This shows once, because the installers aren't from the App Store.

  3. Open your DAW

    Logic and GarageBand find the AU on their next start. Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig and Studio One use the VST3.

macOS 10.13 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel. AU or VST3 host, or the standalone app.

Help

Troubleshooting

It sounds quieter

Removing highs lowers perceived loudness. Raise OUTPUT in the advanced drawer.

The plugin doesn't show up

Check the .vst3 is in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 (Windows) and rescan. On a Mac, run the installer again and restart the DAW.

No name, maker or category in FL Studio

Rescan with Verify plugins ticked in the plugin manager.

macOS says the installer can't be opened

System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. The installers aren't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet.

Windows SmartScreen warns about the app

Click More info, then Run anyway. This only concerns the standalone app.

The window is too big or too small

Drag the bottom-right corner. Every plugin scales from 60% to 200% and remembers the size.

Something else is wrong

E-mail noisedivision@proton.me with the plugin name, version, your DAW and your operating system.

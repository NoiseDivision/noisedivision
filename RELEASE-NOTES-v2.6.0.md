# Noise Division v2.6.0

## TRANSPLANT 1.2.0
- COPY TO...: copy a section's MATCH settings to the sections you pick (or ALL), not only to every section.
- No reference? New MATCH TO: EDM BALANCE (in REFERENCES). Each section of your track is matched to the middle of the EDM tonal balance range shown on METERS. Your references are kept for when you switch back, and adding a reference switches back.

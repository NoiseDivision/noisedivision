Section-aware match EQ

TRANSPLANT

Match EQ, done properly. TRANSPLANT matches your drop to the reference's drop and your break to its break, not one average to another. It moves the reference's bass notes into your key before comparing, so the curve doesn't boost its notes and cut yours. Then it gives you the curve as points you can drag, and the reference to listen to, level-matched, at the same moment of the song.

Overview

What it does

An ordinary match EQ averages a whole reference and a whole track and draws the difference. That's rarely what you want: an intro and a drop sound nothing alike, and two songs in different keys have their bass notes at different frequencies, so the curve ends up boosting the reference's notes and cutting yours. TRANSPLANT reads the structure of both songs (intro, build, drop, break, outro) and their keys. Each section of yours is matched to the same kind of section in the reference, and below KEY ZONE the reference's low end is moved into your key before anything is compared.

The result is a curve made of points: precise by default, with SMOOTH to turn it into broad strokes (fewer, wider points). Drag any point, add your own, and the ones you've touched stay put. Each section keeps its own points and its own MATCH settings, so an intro can get a light touch while the drop gets the full match. Press REF to hear the matching part of the reference at the same moment, turned to your track's loudness, and open METERS to compare loudness, true peak, width and tonal balance side by side.

  • Section by section: your drop matched to its drop, your break to its break. The curve follows the song as it plays
  • Key-aware low end: the reference's bass notes moved into your key before comparing
  • Points you can edit: drag, add, remove. Each section keeps its own points and MATCH settings
  • LISTEN to the reference's matching part at the same moment, LEVEL MATCHED to your track

Under the hood

How it works

What happens to the sound, and how the curve is made.

  1. Analysis

    Both songs are cut into half-second slices: their spectrum (1/24 octave), loudness and notes. From these come the sections (by changes in loudness) and the keys.

  2. Key

    Below KEY ZONE, the reference's spectrum is moved by the key shift, so its bass notes land where yours are.

  3. Difference

    Section against matching section (or the middle of the EDM range, with EDM BALANCE): the reference minus yours, level-matched in the mids, with narrow peaks and notches tamed. Nothing is boosted where your track has nothing.

  4. Points

    SMOOTH sets how detailed the curve is; points are picked where it bends. Your own points are merged in.

  5. FULLNESS

    Per section: the difference between the equal-loudness curves at your loudest level and at this section's level, scaled by FULLNESS, added to the curve.

  6. EQ

    The curve becomes a linear phase (or minimum phase, NATURAL) filter, crossfaded when it changes.

  7. Output

    OUTPUT, then BYPASS or the reference (with LISTEN), crossfaded. Last, HEAR ON, only while you listen.

Quick start

Up and running

  1. Put TRANSPLANT on your master bus, after your mix processing and before the limiter.
  2. Drop a reference track (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 or OGG) on the display, or click REFERENCE. No reference? Choose EDM BALANCE in REFERENCES.
  3. Press LEARN, play your track once from the start, then press STOP. (Or load a bounce of your track from YOUR TRACK.)
  4. Play your song. The curve follows the section that's playing. Start with the Gentle Polish preset, or lower AMOUNT.
  5. Click REF (bottom right) to hear the reference at the same moment and the same loudness, then YOURS to come back.

Workflow

Matching a track

TRANSPLANT needs to hear both songs once. After that, the curve is yours to shape.

  1. Choose a reference in the same genre and at a similar tempo, ideally a finished master you'd like yours to sit next to. Up to three can be loaded; they're moved into your key and averaged.
  2. Let it learn your track. Press LEARN and play the song through once from the very start, then STOP. TRANSPLANT finds your sections and your key. It keeps them with the project, so this is a one-time step (do it again after big changes to the mix).
  3. Check the match. Open SECTIONS: each of your sections shows which part of the reference it's matched to. AUTO picks the same kind (a drop to a drop); pick another part from the list if you like, or BYPASS a section to leave it flat. Open KEY to check both keys and the shift.
  4. Shape the curve. Start with Gentle Polish (AMOUNT 50%, broad, ±6 dB). SMOOTH to the right for broad strokes, to the left for detail. Drag a point to change it (Shift: gain only), double-click to add or remove one. Points you touched are white and stay put. The MATCH knobs belong to the section on the display, so each section can have its own AMOUNT, SMOOTH and so on (COPY TO... copies them to the sections you pick).
  5. Listen. Flip between YOURS and REF while the song plays: the reference plays from the matching section, at the same moment, level-matched. Then BYPASS TRANSPLANT to hear what the curve did.
  6. Compare on the meters. Open METERS: loudness, true peak, punch (PLR), width and the tonal balance of both, with the range typical of loud electronic masters behind them.

Reference

Every control

Getting started

REFERENCEup to 3 tracks

The track to match. WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 or OGG. Several are moved into your key and averaged. TRANSPLANT keeps its analysis with your project; the file itself is only needed for listening.

MATCH TOReference · EDM Balance · default Reference

In REFERENCES. EDM BALANCE needs no reference: each section of your track is matched to the middle of the EDM tonal balance range (the shaded range on METERS). LISTEN: REF and LEVEL MATCH are off then. Your references are kept for when you switch back; adding one switches back.

LEARN

Press it, play your track once from the start, press again (STOP). TRANSPLANT hears its spectrum, sections and key. Or load a bounce of your track from YOUR TRACK.

Presets7 factory presets

Set how the curve is made (smoothing, range, from/to, phase), for every section. They don't touch your references, your track or your points.

Match (per section)

Which section

These settings belong to the section on the display, named at the top right of the drawer. Each section keeps its own; pick another section on the timeline to see and change its settings. A preset sets them for every section.

AMOUNT0 – 150% · default 100%

How much of the analysed curve is applied. Points you moved or added keep their exact values.

SMOOTH1/24 – 1.4 oct · default 1/7 oct

Left: surgical, every 1/24 octave, many points. Right: broad strokes, fewer and wider points. Points you moved stay.

RANGE±3 – ±24 dB · default ±12 dB

The most the curve may boost or cut anywhere.

FROM20 Hz – 2 kHz · default 20 Hz

No matching below this frequency (it fades in over a third of an octave).

TO200 Hz – 20 kHz · default 20 kHz

No matching above this frequency. Around 250 Hz matches only the low end.

FULLNESS0 – 100% · default 0%

Gives back what your ear stops hearing when music is quieter: low end first, a little air too (the equal-loudness curves of ISO 226). The quieter this section is than your loudest one, the more it gets, so a breakdown can feel as full as the drop. Below 30 Hz it adds nothing. With it on, the display shows the whole EQ as a dashed line (WITH FULLNESS), and LEVEL MATCH takes it into account.

OUTPUT−12 – +12 dB · default 0 dB

The level after the EQ. The curve is level-matched in the mids, so this is for fine matching. Also in the header: drag, or double-click for 0 dB.

PHASELinear · Natural · default Linear

LINEAR: no phase shift at all, the low end stays tight, about 0.18 s of latency. NATURAL: like an analogue or ordinary EQ, almost no latency, for tracking or live use. One setting for the whole song.

RESET POINTS

Puts the points of the section you're looking at back to the analysis.

COPY TO...

Copies the settings of the section on the display (AMOUNT, SMOOTH, RANGE, FROM, TO, OUTPUT) to the sections you pick, or ALL. Your points stay.

Key

KEY MODEAuto · Manual · Off · default Auto

AUTO reads both keys and moves the reference's low end the shorter way round. MANUAL: you set the shift. OFF: the low end is compared as it is, like an ordinary match EQ.

SHIFT−6 – +6 semitones

With MANUAL: how far the reference's low end is moved.

KEY ZONE80 Hz – 1 kHz · default 300 Hz

Up to where the low end is moved. Above it (plus half an octave to fade) the reference is compared as it is.

Sections

Match per sectionAuto or a part of the reference

Which part of the reference each section of yours is matched to. AUTO: the same kind (a drop to a drop), the nearest stand-in if the reference has none.

BYPASS (per section)On / Off

No EQ in this section.

FOLLOW PLAYHEADOn / Off · default On

The curve changes with the section that's playing, by song position, crossfaded. Off: the section you click on the timeline is used for the whole song.

ONE CURVE FOR ALLOn / Off · default Off

Forget the sections: one curve from the whole track (its loudest half) against the whole reference.

The curve

Drag a point

Moves it. Hold Shift to change only its gain.

Double-click

On the curve: adds a point. On a point: removes it.

Right-click

Remove a point, put it back to the analysis, or reset all points in this section or in every section.

White points

Points you moved or added. They keep their values when SMOOTH, AMOUNT or the analysis changes.

Listening and comparing

YOURS · REFdefault YOURS

REF plays the reference instead, from the part that matches what's playing, at the same moment (in your drop you hear its drop). It plays while your DAW plays.

LEVEL MATCHOn / Off · default On

Turns the reference to your track's loudness in each section, so you compare tone, not volume. It starts from the analysis and is fine-tuned as the song plays.

HEAR ONStudio · Club · Car · Laptop · Phone · Earbuds · Quiet room

Bottom left. Roughly how your track, and the reference with REF, would sound on that system at its usual level: a club's big sub at about 100 dB, a car's boomy cabin, a laptop or a phone with no low end (in mono), earbuds, or a quiet room. Levelled in the mids so you compare tone, not volume. For your ears only: it is never in a bounce, and it goes back to Studio when a project opens. These are typical systems, not measurements of any product.

METERS

Short-term and section loudness, true peak, PLR, width and tonal balance, for your track and the reference.

BYPASSOn / Off

The original sound, with the same latency, so A/B is fair.

A · B

Two versions of the settings and points. Right-click to copy one to the other.

Undo · Redo

For every change to the curve and the settings.

Every plugin window scales from 60% to 200%: drag the bottom-right corner. Double-click a knob to return it to its default.

Tips

Getting the most out of it

  • Start gentle: Gentle Polish, or AMOUNT 30–60%. A match EQ shows you where to go; you rarely need all the way.
  • Low end in a different key? Leave KEY MODE on AUTO. If the reading is wrong (check KEY), set the shift with MANUAL.
  • Give intros and breaks a lighter AMOUNT than the drops: each section keeps its own MATCH settings.
  • Breakdown sounds thin next to the drop? Try FULLNESS at 30–60% on it. It gives back what your ear loses at the lower level, without touching the drop.
  • Before you bounce, flip HEAR ON through Club, Car and Phone with REF: if your low end vanishes on the phone or booms in the car and the reference's doesn't, you know where to look.
  • No reference to hand? Choose EDM BALANCE in REFERENCES and start with Gentle Polish: each section moves towards the middle of the range on METERS.
  • Only want the bass balance? Use Low End Only, or set TO to about 250 Hz.
  • Flip YOURS and REF on the loudest part of the song. With LEVEL MATCH on, any difference you hear is tone, not volume.
  • Use the tonal balance on METERS to check yours against the range, not only against one reference.
  • Learned the track, then changed the mix a lot? Press LEARN again, or the curve will correct things that are already fixed.
  • TRANSPLANT has about 0.18 s of latency in LINEAR phase. Your DAW compensates on playback; for recording, switch to NATURAL or bypass it.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Windows

  1. Unzip

    Open the downloaded .zip. Inside is the plugin (.vst3).

  2. Copy the .vst3

    Move the .vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. Every Windows DAW looks there.

  3. Rescan

    Let your DAW scan for new plugins. The plugin shows up under Noise Division.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. VST3 host.

macOS

  1. Open the installer

    Double-click the .pkg. It installs the Audio Unit and the VST3.

  2. If macOS blocks it

    Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. This shows once, because the installers aren't from the App Store.

  3. Open your DAW

    Logic and GarageBand find the AU on their next start. Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig and Studio One use the VST3.

macOS 10.13 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel. AU or VST3 host.

Help

Troubleshooting

The curve doesn't change between sections

FOLLOW PLAYHEAD (in SECTIONS) must be on, and the song has to start where it started when TRANSPLANT learned it: the sections are found by song position. If you moved the song, press LEARN again.

It boosts or cuts too much

Lower AMOUNT or RANGE, or turn SMOOTH to the right. Gentle Polish is a good start.

Turning AMOUNT changes only one section

That's on purpose: the MATCH settings belong to the section on the display. Click COPY TO... and pick the sections that should get the same settings, or pick a preset.

My bounce sounds different from what I heard

HEAR ON was probably on. It only changes what you hear: bounces (offline) never include it. Set it to Studio while mixing, and if your DAW bounces in real time, set it to Studio before the bounce too.

The low end sounds wrong

Check the keys in KEY. If one is misread, choose MANUAL and set the shift, or OFF to compare the low end as it is.

REF is silent or says FILE MISSING

The reference file has moved or been renamed. Load it again from REFERENCES. The analysis is kept with the project, so the curve doesn't change.

No curve, only an empty display

TRANSPLANT needs both: a reference (or EDM BALANCE in REFERENCES), and your track (press LEARN and play it from the start, or load a bounce).

It's late against the other tracks

Your DAW should compensate the latency (about 0.18 s in LINEAR). If it doesn't (some live or low-latency modes), switch PHASE to NATURAL.

The plugin doesn't show up

Check the .vst3 is in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 (Windows) and rescan. On a Mac, run the installer again and restart the DAW.

No name, maker or category in FL Studio

Rescan with Verify plugins ticked in the plugin manager.

macOS says the installer can't be opened

System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. The installers aren't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet.

The window is too big or too small

Drag the bottom-right corner. Every plugin scales from 60% to 200% and remembers the size.

Something else is wrong

E-mail noisedivision@proton.me with the plugin name, version, your DAW and your operating system.

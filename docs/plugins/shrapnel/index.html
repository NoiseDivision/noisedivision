Overview

What it does

SHRAPNEL is a spectral distortion. Normal distortion bends the waveform, which also creates intermodulation (muddy, out-of-tune tones on chords) and aliasing. SHRAPNEL works in the frequency domain instead: it splits the sound into hundreds of frequency bins and distorts the spectrum itself.

That makes new sounds possible. HARMONICS adds harmonics at the exact pitch of every tone, so chords stay clean. CRUSH turns the spectrum into blocks, SMEAR blurs it over time, SCRAMBLE turns tones into breath and noise, and WARP shifts partials off their harmonics into metal and bells. The split controls choose how much the tones, the noise and the transients get hit, and the drive curve chooses which frequencies.

  • Six modes: HARMONICS, CLIP, CRUSH, SMEAR, SCRAMBLE and WARP, each with its own CHARACTER control
  • Clean harmonics: chords stay clear with no intermodulation, and nothing aliases
  • Split: choose how much the tones, the noise and the transients get hit, so drums can stay punchy
  • Drive curve with up to 8 draggable points: smash the mids, spare the sub
  • Level stays put: DRIVE changes the character, not the volume. Dynamic drive, tone, mix and output clip
  • 12 factory presets with descriptions, including Bass Thickener, Lo-Fi Robot and Metallic Drums

Quick start

Up and running

  1. Pick a MODE, or start from a preset.
  2. Turn up DRIVE. The level stays about the same, so you hear the character change, not a volume jump.
  3. Set CHARACTER: its meaning changes with the mode (shown above the knob).
  4. Use the split faders: lower TRANSIENT to keep hits clean, lower NOISE to leave breath and hiss alone.
  5. Double-click the display to add a drive point; drag it down over the sub to spare the low end.

Reference

Every control

Main

MODE6 modes · default HARMONICS

The kind of spectral distortion. See the modes below.

DRIVE0 – 100% · default 50%

How hard the sound is hit. The output level stays about the same.

CHARACTER0 – 100% · default 50%

Shapes the distortion. What it does depends on the mode (shown above the knob).

TONE−100 – +100 · default 0

Tilts the distorted sound darker (left) or brighter (right).

Split

TONAL0 – 100% · default 90%

How much the tones (notes, harmonics, resonances) get distorted.

NOISE0 – 100% · default 50%

How much the noisy parts (breath, air, hiss, cymbal wash) get distorted.

TRANSIENT0 – 100% · default 20%

How much the attacks get distorted. Keep it low to leave hits clean and punchy.

DYNAMIC0 – 100% · default 50%

Louder parts get hit harder, quieter parts less, like analogue gear. 0% hits everything the same.

Drive curve

Pointsup to 8

Double-click the display to add a point. Drag it up to hit that area harder, down to spare it. Double-click or right-click a point to remove it.

Output

MIX0 – 100% · default 100%

Blends the untouched sound back in.

GAIN−24 – +24 dB · default 0

Output level.

CLIPOFF · SOFT · HARD · default SOFT

SOFT rounds off peaks above −0.1 dBFS; HARD cuts them flat there. Extreme settings can throw out peaks, so SOFT is on by default.

Routing and quality

STEREO · L / R · M / Sdefault STEREO

STEREO processes both sides together so the image stays put and mono-compatible. L / R and M / S process the two channels separately.

QUALITYLOW LAT · NORMAL · HIGH · default NORMAL

About 11, 21 or 43 ms. LOW LAT is tightest on drums; HIGH has the finest frequency detail for bass and HARMONICS on low notes.

Display

Spectrum

Input in grey, distorted output from red to amber. Shards fly off the hardest-hit peaks. The amber dashed line is the drive curve.

Every plugin window scales from 60% to 200%: drag the bottom-right corner. Double-click a knob to return it to its default.

Modes

6 ways to break it

CHARACTER changes its job with the mode; the label above the knob shows what it does.

HARMONICS

Character: even ↔ odd

Adds 2nd to 8th harmonics to every tone, at its exact pitch. Thick and warm, but chords stay clean (no intermodulation) and nothing folds back as aliasing. CHARACTER goes from even (warm, tube) to odd (hard, buzzy).

CLIP

Character: tight ↔ flat

Flattens the spectrum: quiet partials and noise are pulled up to meet the loud ones. Aggressive and in your face. CHARACTER goes from tight (keeps the shape of the sound) to flat (a wall of grit).

CRUSH

Character: block size

A spectral bitcrusher. Levels snap to coarse steps and neighbouring frequencies merge into blocks: steppy, robotic, lo-fi, without the usual aliasing whistle.

SMEAR

Character: spread

Frequencies are held and blurred over time: washed-out, broken-tape and reverb-like textures. CHARACTER sets how far it also blurs across frequency.

SCRAMBLE

Character: gate

Randomises the phases: breathy and whispery at first, then noise shaped like the sound. CHARACTER also gates frequencies at random for a sparkly, glitchy texture.

WARP

Character: squeeze ↔ stretch

Shifts the spectrum up and stretches or squeezes it, so harmonics stop lining up: metallic, bell-like, like ring modulation but smoother. CHARACTER: squeeze (left), shift only (middle), stretch (right).

Tips

Getting the most out of it

  • For bass that cuts through on phones, use HARMONICS with a drive point pulled down below 80 Hz: the sub stays clean and the harmonics carry the note.
  • Keep TRANSIENT low on drums so the hits stay punchy while the tails get the distortion.
  • SMEAR and SCRAMBLE at 30–60% MIX make great textures on pads and vocals without losing the original.
  • Automate MODE between sections for sudden changes in texture.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Windows

  1. Unzip

    Open the downloaded .zip. Inside is the plugin (.vst3) and a standalone app.

  2. Copy the .vst3

    Move the .vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. Every Windows DAW looks there.

  3. Rescan

    Let your DAW scan for new plugins. The plugin shows up under Noise Division.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. VST3 host or the standalone app.

macOS

  1. Open the installer

    Double-click the .pkg. It installs the Audio Unit, the VST3 and the standalone app.

  2. If macOS blocks it

    Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. This shows once, because the installers aren't from the App Store.

  3. Open your DAW

    Logic and GarageBand find the AU on their next start. Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig and Studio One use the VST3.

macOS 10.13 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel. AU or VST3 host, or the standalone app.

Help

Troubleshooting

Peaks jump at extreme settings

Keep CLIP on SOFT (the default), or lower GAIN.

Bass sounds smeared

Use HIGH quality for low notes, or LOW LAT for tight drums.

The plugin doesn't show up

Check the .vst3 is in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 (Windows) and rescan. On a Mac, run the installer again and restart the DAW.

No name, maker or category in FL Studio

Rescan with Verify plugins ticked in the plugin manager.

macOS says the installer can't be opened

System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. The installers aren't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet.

Windows SmartScreen warns about the app

Click More info, then Run anyway. This only concerns the standalone app.

The window is too big or too small

Drag the bottom-right corner. Every plugin scales from 60% to 200% and remembers the size.

Something else is wrong

E-mail noisedivision@proton.me with the plugin name, version, your DAW and your operating system.
